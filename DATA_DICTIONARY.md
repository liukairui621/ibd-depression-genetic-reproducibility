# Derived-result data dictionary

The files under `results/derived/` are machine-readable outputs generated by
the archived scripts. They contain aggregate or summary-level results only.

## `global_core`

- `h2_summary.tsv`: LDSC univariate SNP-heritability and quality-control fields.
- `rg_summary.tsv`: pairwise genetic correlations, standard errors, P values,
  multiple-testing values, and cross-trait intercept diagnostics.
- `primary_pair_summary.tsv`: the two reciprocal genome-wide estimates,
  reported separately.
- `primary_pair_dependence_diagnostic.tsv`: GenomicSEM sampling covariance and
  correlation demonstrating why the reciprocal estimates were not pooled.
- `layer1_primary_decision.tsv`: prespecified Layer-1 decision record.

## `local`

- `univ_all_primary.tsv`: LAVA local univariate results used for the
  estimability gate.
- `bivar_all_primary.tsv`: all eligible LAVA bivariate tests.
- `bivar_q05_primary.tsv`: bivariate tests passing BH q<0.05 across 110 tests.
- `local_reproducibility.tsv`: cross-pair region-overlap and direction checks.
- `same_block_eligible_both.tsv`: blocks estimable in both reciprocal pairs.
- `local_estimability.tsv`: analyzable-block counts by trait.
- `layer2_decision.tsv`: prespecified Layer-2 stopping decision.
- `layer2_r_verification.tsv` and `local_independent_verification.tsv`:
  independent numerical verification of the corrected 110-test family in R.
- `replicated_local_components.tsv`: header-only result documenting zero
  components meeting the reproduction rule.

## `global_extension`

- `h2_summary.tsv`: LDSC quality-control fields for extension traits.
- `rg_ukb_definition_matrix.tsv`: the 24 UK Biobank depression-definition by
  IBD-family cells, including FDR and intercept flags.
- `attribution/*.tsv`: adjusted marginals, empirical dispersion, covariance-
  aware contrasts, omnibus tests, interactions, leave-one-out summaries, and
  the frozen classification.

## `local_power`

- `approximate_detectable_rho.tsv`: per-block descriptive normal-theory
  minimum-detectable-correlation calculation.
- `approximate_detectable_rho_summary.tsv`: pair-level medians and ranges.

## `qc_sensitivity`

- `sensitivity_summary.tsv`: summary after excluding six cross-trait-intercept
  flagged cells.
- `retained_cells.tsv` and `excluded_flagged_cells.tsv`: exact cells entering
  or excluded from the sensitivity analysis.
- `adjusted_marginal_rg.tsv`, `omnibus_tests.tsv`, and `interaction_tests.tsv`:
  covariance-aware sensitivity outputs.

Column names are preserved from the analysis outputs. See the Methods and the
two frozen protocols for estimands, thresholds, and interpretation rules.
